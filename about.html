About

DFMA Review Studio is a free tool for mechanical engineers. It checks 3D models for manufacturability (CNC machining, 3D printing and casting), reviews engineering drawings against ISO rules, works out tolerances, fits and stack-ups, and helps with design for assembly.

Who makes it

Shahid Fardeen builds DFMA Review Studio at YZ Labs. The goal is simple: give every engineer a free second check on parts and drawings, so problems are caught on the screen and not on the shop floor.

Shahid Fardeen on LinkedIn

Your files stay on your device

Models and drawings are read and checked inside your web browser. They are not uploaded to our server. The AI engineer copies your question and opens the AI agent you choose (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity) in a new tab, signed in with your own account. Nothing is sent unless you paste it there, or unless you add your own Anthropic API key.

Support the project

DFMA Review Studio is free and has no sign-up. If it helps your work, you can support hosting and new checks with a small tip.

Accuracy

Checks use typical shop capability and published standards (ISO 2768, ISO 286, ISO 1101 and others). Treat the results as a guide and confirm critical values with the standard and your supplier.
