Types of machining machines and their tolerances

Lathes, mills and machining centres, EDM, grinders, CMMs and finishing equipment with typical tolerances.

Machines used in precision manufacturing

Typical tolerances are a guide; a good shop on a good machine can do better

Lathes and turning

MachineWhat it doesTypical tolerance
Engine lathe (manual)General-purpose manual lathe driven by handwheels, leadscrew and gear train.±0.02 to 0.05 mm
Bench and toolroom latheSmall bench lathes for light work; toolroom lathes built tighter for jigs, fixtures and tools.±0.01 to 0.02 mm
Turret / capstan latheA multi-tool turret replaces the tailstock for fast repeat operations.±0.02 to 0.05 mm
NC latheBasic numerical control automates repeat turning cycles.±0.02 mm
2-axis CNC latheX and Z axes: outside and inside turning, facing, grooving, threading.±0.01 to 0.05 mm
CNC lathe with live tooling (C-axis)Driven tools mill, drill and tap flats and cross holes on turned parts.±0.01 to 0.05 mm
Mill-turn centre (4 to 6 axes)Y-axis, sub-spindle or twin turrets finish complex parts in one setup.±0.01 mm
Swiss-type (sliding headstock) latheBar slides through a guide bush; ideal for long slender precision parts such as medical screws.±0.002 to 0.01 mm
Multi-spindle latheSeveral spindles cut at once for very high volume turned parts.±0.01 to 0.05 mm
Vertical turning centre (VTC)Vertical spindle carries large heavy rings and discs.±0.01 to 0.03 mm

Milling and machining centres

MachineWhat it doesTypical tolerance
Knee-and-column (Bridgeport-type) millClassic manual mill; the table rises on a knee.±0.02 to 0.05 mm
Horizontal and universal millHorizontal spindle with arbor cutters for heavy slab and slot cuts; universal mills swivel the table for helical work.±0.02 to 0.05 mm
Bed-type millTable on a fixed heavy bed for rigidity.±0.02 mm
Vertical machining centre (VMC), 3-axisCNC mill with a vertical spindle and automatic tool changer. The standard choice for prismatic parts machined from one or two sides.±0.01 to 0.05 mm
4-axis and 3+2 machiningA rotary axis turns the part between cuts, so more sides are reached in one setup.±0.01 to 0.03 mm
5-axis machining centreTwo rotary axes move continuously for impellers, blades and organic shapes.±0.01 to 0.03 mm
Horizontal machining centre (HMC)Horizontal spindle and pallet changer; excellent chip removal for box-shaped parts.±0.01 to 0.05 mm
Gantry / bridge millLarge bridge structure for big aerospace and automotive frames.±0.02 to 0.1 mm
CNC routerFast light machine for wood, plastics and soft metals.±0.1 to 0.25 mm

Special and non-conventional

MachineWhat it doesTypical tolerance
Wire EDMA thin wire erodes hardened metal by sparks; sharp inside corners and punch profiles.±0.002 to 0.005 mm
Sinker EDMA shaped electrode erodes mould cavities and blind shapes.±0.005 mm
Laser, plasma and waterjet cuttingProfile cutting of plate and sheet. Waterjet leaves no heat-affected zone.laser ±0.05 to 0.1, plasma ±0.3 to 1.5, waterjet about ±0.03 to 0.1 mm
CNC punch press and press brakePunching holes in sheet, then bending to shape.±0.1 mm, ±0.5° bends
Gear hobbing, shaping and grindingCut and finish gear teeth.grinding ±0.001 to 0.01 mm

Grinding

MachineWhat it doesTypical tolerance
Surface grinder, horizontal spindleWheel periphery over a reciprocating magnetic chuck for flat precision faces.±0.002 to 0.005 mm
Surface grinder, vertical spindleWheel face removes more stock over flat plates.±0.005 mm
Cylindrical grinderPart turns between centres while the wheel grinds the outside diameter.±0.002 mm
Centreless grinderPart runs between a regulating wheel and a grinding wheel, no centre holes needed; high volume pins.±0.002 mm
Internal and jig grindersGrind bores and precise hole positions.±0.002 mm
Tool and cutter grinderMakes and resharpens drills and end mills.–
Creep-feed grinderDeep cuts in one slow pass for profiles in hard metals.±0.005 mm

Measuring (CMM)

MachineWhat it doesTypical tolerance
Bridge CMMMoving bridge over a granite table probes the part in 3D. The most common type.about 0.002 to 0.005 mm
Cantilever CMMOne-sided arm for small, easy-to-reach parts.about 0.002 mm
Gantry CMMVery large frame for car bodies and aircraft structures.about 0.01 to 0.02 mm
Portable arm and laser scannerHand-held arm or tracker on the shop floor for inspection and reverse engineering.about 0.02 to 0.05 mm

Finishing

MachineWhat it doesTypical tolerance
Deburring and chamfer toolsChamfer mills and hand deburring knives break sharp edges.–
Vibratory and barrel tumblersMedia in a vibrating tub deburr and polish batches of small parts.–
Media blastingSand, glass bead or ceramic blasting for texture and cleaning.–
Anodising and passivationChemical baths add an oxide layer (aluminium) or clean the surface for corrosion resistance (stainless).–
ElectropolishingRemoves microscopic peaks electrochemically for a bright smooth finish.–
