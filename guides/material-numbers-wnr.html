European material numbers (WNr, EN 10027-2) explained

How material numbers like 1.4301 work, the number ranges for cast iron, steel, copper, nickel, aluminium and titanium.

How European material numbers work

Every metal in Europe has a material number (Werkstoffnummer, WNr) under EN 10027-2, written like 1.4301. The first digit is the main group, the next two digits the type group, and the last two (or more) a running number. So 1.4301 reads: 1 = steel, 43 = stainless steel group, 01 = the grade (known as 304). Tens of thousands of numbers are registered, but they all follow this structure.
