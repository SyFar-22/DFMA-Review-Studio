Modifiers

Material condition modifiers

SymbolModifierMeaningUsed with
ⓂMaximum material condition (MMC)Applies when the feature holds the most material: smallest hole or largest pin. Bonus tolerance is added as the made size moves toward LMC, which lowers cost.Features of size only
ⓁLeast material condition (LMC)Applies when the feature holds the least material: largest hole or smallest pin. Bonus grows as the size moves toward MMC. Used to protect minimum wall thickness.Features of size only
no symbolRegardless of feature size (RFS)The tolerance stays as stated whatever size the feature is made at. Default in ASME Y14.5 and ISO, so no symbol is shown. ASME 1994 drawings may show Ⓢ.Default

Other common modifiers

SymbolModifierMeaningUsed with
ⓅProjected tolerance zoneProjects the zone outside the part by a stated height. Used on threaded and press-fit holes so the mating bolt or pin does not clash.Position and orientation
ⓉTangent planeOnly the plane touching the high points of the surface is controlled, not the whole surface.Orientation of flat surfaces
ⒻFree stateTolerance applies with the part relaxed and unrestrained. For flexible parts such as rubber or thin plastic.Any, on non-rigid parts
STStatistical toleranceTolerance is met by statistical process control rather than absolute worst-case limits.Any, with a statistical note
CFContinuous featureSeveral separated features are treated as one continuous surface or feature of size.Features of size and profile
ⓊUnequally disposed profileThe profile zone is split unequally either side of the true profile; the value after Ⓤ is the amount outside the material. ISO writes UZ.Profile only
CZCombined (common) zoneOne common tolerance zone applies to several separate features. ISO notation.ISO, several features
In the tolerance compartment| ⌖ | Ø0.2 Ⓜ | A | B | C |

Placed right after the value. It changes the tolerance of the feature itself, for example bonus tolerance at MMC.

In a datum compartment| ⌖ | Ø0.2 Ⓜ | A | B Ⓜ | C |

Placed after the datum letter. It allows datum shift: the part may move on a datum feature of size that is made away from its MMC.
